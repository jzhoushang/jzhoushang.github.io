The Ping Pong Club is a non-profit organization based in Concord, MA which raises money for Mass General Hospital and Dana Farber via community ping pong tournaments and other outreach events. Our online presence provides information about upcoming events, past achievements, and an online store used to sell merchandise.

As the Technical Director, I led the development of the organization website from scratch. The project was started from nothing: a small team of myself and another programmer, both having to learn web development from scratch; no previous website or design plans; and very little direction from an at-the-time still young club. 

The project uses [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/), a static site generator, and the Hugo theme [Tella](https://themes.gohugo.io/themes/tella/). Most of the site is written in the easy-to-use Markdown for posterity's sake, but some of our specific needs required editing of the HTML templates from which the site is generated. [Square](https://squareup.com) is used for the online store.
