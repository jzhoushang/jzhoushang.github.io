The [three-body problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three-body_problem) involves three point-like bodies with mass that apply gravitational forces to each other. This is an example of a chaotic system, where a small change in the initial conditions results in extremely different patterns.

A simulation of the three-body problem was chosen to be the background of this website's home screen because it's simple, easy for most devices to handle, and the chaotic nature means that every refresh will create a unique pattern. The simulation on the home page is a modified version, where forces are dampened to keep the bodies on screen and moving in interesting patterns as opposed to flying off into empty space (which creates more visually interesting patterns). The masses and locations of each point mass are randomized.
